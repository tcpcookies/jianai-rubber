A Shenzhen factory built around soft materials

Shenzhen Jianai Rubber Products Co., Ltd. — 深圳市简爱橡胶制品有限公司 — makes silicone and rubber goods in Shenzhen, Guangdong, and supplies adult wellness items to buyers and brands outside China.

Who we are

Soft-material manufacturing, not trading

We are a manufacturing company, not an intermediary. Our work centres on silicone and TPR: forming the soft part, fitting the electronics inside it, sealing it, testing it, and packing it in a box that can go straight onto a retail shelf or into a marketplace fulfilment centre.

That focus shapes how we quote. When a buyer asks for a colour change, a different shore hardness or a modified motor profile, the answer comes from the production floor rather than from a supplier list.

Most of our customers are importers, distributors and private-label brands who need a factory that can hold a specification across repeat orders — the same silicone feel, the same trim colour, the same box, order after order.

How we work

Three things buyers tell us matter

01 · Consistency

The same product twice

Colour, hardness and finish are recorded on the specification sheet, so a repeat order matches the first one instead of drifting. This is the single biggest reason buyers stay with a factory.

02 · Honesty

A date we can hold

Lead times are quoted against the actual material and mould situation at the time of order, not against an optimistic standard. If something slips, we say so early rather than at the shipping deadline.

03 · Discretion

Your brand stays yours

We do not publish customer brands, sell your mould to a third party, or reuse your artwork. NDA before technical exchange is normal practice for us, not an exception.

Compliance

Regulatory responsibility, split clearly

Adult products are regulated differently in every market. We manufacture to the material and documentation requirements you specify, and supply the test reports and declarations that can be issued for the materials used.

Market registration, labelling rules, packaging warnings and import clearance for the destination country remain the buyer's responsibility — and we will tell you at quotation stage which documents we can and cannot provide for your market, rather than promising paperwork we cannot issue.
