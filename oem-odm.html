OEM & ODM for adult wellness brands

Two ways to work with us: take an existing platform and put your brand, colour and packaging on it — or bring a design and we develop the mould, the electronics and the finished item.

OEM — your brand on a proven model

Fastest route to market

Choose a model from our current range. We change the colour, the silicone finish, the logo placement and the packaging, then produce to your order quantity. No tooling cost on the body; the shortest path from enquiry to a sellable SKU.

  • 1

    Colour & finish

    Matched to your reference or Pantone, including metal-effect trim.

  • 2

    Logo

    Pad printing, laser marking or debossed mark on the product and case.

  • 3

    Packaging

    Gift box, insert, manual, barcode and carton marks to your artwork.

ODM — we develop it with you

For proprietary designs

Send a 3D file, a sketch or a competitor sample. We quote tooling and a first-article sample, then iterate on the mould until the physical part matches the drawing before mass production is released.

  • 1

    Mould development

    New tooling for a shape, structure or size that does not exist in our range.

  • 2

    Electronics specification

    Motor type, stroke length, heating element, PCB and battery matched to your function list.

  • 3

    Sample rounds

    Physical samples for approval, revised until signed off — before the production run.

Capability

What stays inside our factory

Keeping moulding, assembly and packing under one roof is what makes short runs and quick colour changes possible.

  • 01

    Silicone and TPR moulding

    Compression and injection moulding of soft parts, with shore hardness and surface texture controlled per batch.

  • 02

    Assembly and sealing

    Motor, PCB, battery and charging contacts installed and sealed; units soak-tested before the next stage.

  • 03

    Function and charge testing

    Every unit charged, run through its modes and inspected for appearance and finish before packing.

  • 04

    Packing and documentation

    Retail-ready or bulk packing, plus the invoice, packing list and test paperwork your forwarder or marketplace requires.

Commercial

Order terms, plainly stated

Quantity

Minimum order quantity is set per model, per colour and per packaging format — a new tool or a fully custom gift box changes the break-even point. Send your target quantity and we will tell you honestly whether it works or what the nearest workable quantity is.

Lead time

Lead time is quoted per order, because it depends on whether tooling is needed, whether materials are in stock and how complex the packaging is. The confirmed date is written into the contract, not left verbal.

Payment

Standard practice is a deposit with the balance settled before shipment, confirmed in the proforma invoice. Terms for repeat customers are agreed case by case.

Shipping

We deliver to the forwarder or express account you nominate. If you do not have one, we can quote door-to-door options for your destination at the time of order.

Confidentiality & tooling ownership

Your drawings, artwork and any mould developed specifically for you remain your property. An NDA can be signed before technical files are exchanged, and tooling ownership is stated in the contract.
