What we do

A rubber and silicone factory that also understands retail

Most adult-product factories stop at the moulded part. We build the finished, chargeable, packaged item — and we build it to the specification sheet your buyer will actually check.

    Material and mould development

Buyer questions

Before you send an enquiry

Can you produce under our own brand?

Yes. Private label is our standard mode of work: your logo on the product, your gift box, your manual and your barcode. Artwork can be prepared by us from your brand files, or supplied print-ready by your designer.

Do you supply samples before an order?

Samples of current models can be sent for evaluation. Sample cost, courier account and whether the sample charge is credited against the first order are confirmed in the quotation, per model.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the model, the colour and whether new tooling or new packaging is required. Tell us your target quantity and market, and we will confirm what is workable for that specific item.

Which documents can you provide for customs and retail?

Commercial invoice, packing list and the material or test documentation requested by your market can be prepared. Please state your destination country and channel at the enquiry stage so the correct paperwork is quoted.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Drawings, moulds developed for you and brand artwork are treated as customer property. An NDA can be signed before technical files are exchanged.

Tell us the model, the market and the quantity.

We will come back with options, pricing and a realistic lead time — no catalogue spam.
